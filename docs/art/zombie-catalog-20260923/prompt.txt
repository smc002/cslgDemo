Upper crate lid cracked open with THREE exaggerated oversize pointed gold bullet tips protruding above the shoulder, black cartridge cases with bright gold bands clearly visible. One slack diagonal ammunition belt across chest with 4 chunky rounds, consistent with the same black-and-gold ammo. The backpack crate and protruding bullets dominate readable silhouette, wide horizontal box, NOT a cylindrical tank, not a treasure chest, not coins, no explosives or flames. Crate remains behind body in all views, with straps physically connected.
Style: polished hand-painted cartoon game sprite rendering, bold clean dark outlines, bright saturated accents, readable broad shapes, slightly elevated camera, soft top-left light, similar to a friendly humorous zombie defense mobile game.
